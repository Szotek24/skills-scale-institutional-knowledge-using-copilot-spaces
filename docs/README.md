# OctoAcme Project Management Docs README

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management documentation suite. This collection provides concise, actionable guidance for managing projects at OctoAcme, covering the entire lifecycle from initiation through continuous improvement.

## Project Management Processes Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, customer-first project lifecycle that prioritizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. The approach is organized around five key phases:

- **Initiation:** Validate business need, align stakeholders, and define success criteria. Confirm objectives, scope, and measurable outcomes for each new project.
- **Planning:** Develop actionable roadmaps, break work into shippable increments, assign roles, detail deliverables, and forecast required resources.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Monitor progress, adjust plans as needed, manage risks, maintain team rhythm through daily standups and weekly syncs, and keep stakeholders informed.
- **Release & Deployment:** Standardize release procedures, coordinate readiness checks, ensure quality gates are met, and manage smooth rollouts with documented rollback plans.
- **Risks & Communication:** Identify, assess, and mitigate risks while ensuring transparent, layered communication throughout the project. Escalate blockers through defined channels.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement:** Review outcomes, gather feedback, capture lessons learned, and feed improvements directly into operational processes.

### Core Roles and Communication

Three primary personas drive OctoAcme projects:

- **Project Managers** coordinate schedules, risks, and communications to keep delivery on track and maintain transparency.
- **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success through data-informed decisions.
- **Developers** implement features while collaborating on design, testing, and risk identification.

Communication is structured through a regular cadence: daily standups (15 minutes), weekly PM/PdM synchronization, twice-weekly team standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed.

### Execution, Quality, and Risk Management

Day-to-day execution relies on GitHub Projects with a standardized workflow and small, focused pull requests that include clear acceptance criteria. Quality is enforced through unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning in CI. Risk management is continuous—risks are identified, assessed, mitigated through documented action plans, and monitored weekly. When blockers arise, escalation follows a three-level path: team triage → PM escalation to Product Lead → sponsor-level escalation.
